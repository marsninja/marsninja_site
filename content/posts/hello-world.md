Welcome. This is my corner of the internet — a place for longer-form thoughts on
programming languages, AI systems, and the occasional detour into whatever I'm
building at the moment.

## Why a blog in 2026?

Feeds are optimized for takes. Blogs are optimized for *thinking*. I wanted a
place where an idea can take three thousand words if it needs them, where code
can sit next to prose, and where nothing disappears into an algorithmic void
after 48 hours.

> The best way to understand a system is to build a small, honest version of it
> yourself.

That applies to blogs too — this whole site is a couple of files of
[Jac](https://www.jaseci.org/), a directory of markdown, and nothing else. No
CMS, no database, no static-site generator with 400 transitive dependencies.

## What to expect

- **Languages & compilers** — notes from building Jac and its native backend
- **AI-native software** — what programming looks like when models are a
  language feature, not an API call
- **Systems** — runtimes, schedulers, and the occasional performance war story

The [about page](/about) has more on who I am. The source for every post is a
plain `.md` file — if you spot an error, tell me and I'll fix the file.

Thanks for reading.
