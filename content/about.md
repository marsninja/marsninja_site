I'm **marsninja** — I build programming languages, AI runtimes, and the
occasional thing that is neither but should be.

Most of my time goes into [Jac](https://www.jaseci.org/), a language that
treats graphs, scale, and AI models as first-class citizens instead of
libraries. This site is itself a small Jac program — markdown files in, a
full-stack app out. The [second post](/posts/building-this-blog-in-jac)
explains how.

## Elsewhere

- **GitHub** — [github.com/marsninja](https://github.com/marsninja)
- **Jaseci** — [jaseci.org](https://www.jaseci.org/)

## Colophon

Built with Jac and jac-shadcn. Markdown is rendered server-side with Pygments
highlighting; the client ships no markdown parser. Fonts: Geist. Hosting:
wherever `jac start` happens to be running.

Opinions here are my own, cached aggressively, and invalidated often.
